# Abdullah Ahmad Khan — Portfolio Design System

## Direction
Premium research editorial. The site should feel authored by an experienced editorial/web designer, not assembled from UI trends.

## Principles
- Content hierarchy before decoration.
- One warm neutral surface system; no section-by-section colour themes.
- One accent colour used sparingly for emphasis and interaction.
- No gradients, glassmorphism, neon AI styling, particles, scroll hijacking, custom cursors, or decorative science art.
- Motion is optional and functional only; hover/focus transitions stay under 200ms.
- Publications read like an academic index; projects read like case studies; teaching stays secondary.

## Typography
- Display: Newsreader, serif fallback.
- Interface/body: DM Sans, system sans fallback.
- Body minimum: 16px on mobile.
- Desktop reading measure: roughly 60–75 characters.

## Colour
- Paper: #F7F4ED
- Surface: #FCFBF8
- Ink: #171714
- Muted: #6B675F
- Rule: #D9D3C8
- Accent: #7A2535
- Accent dark: #5B1D2A

The accent is intentionally darker than a decorative gold so small text and focus states retain strong contrast on the paper surface.

## Layout
- Max content width: 1240px.
- Desktop: 12-column editorial grid.
- Mobile-first reflow; no horizontal scrolling or nested scroll regions.
- Section rhythm: 96–144px desktop, 72–96px mobile.
- Square corners by default; no gratuitous card radius.

## Interaction
- Native vertical scrolling only.
- Visible keyboard focus.
- Primary interactive targets at least 44px high on touch layouts.
- Native details/summary for secondary teaching material.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Homepage hierarchy
1. Hero / identity and research proposition
2. Research thesis and four principles
3. Three thesis/publication anchors + compact additional research
4. Three selected project case studies + compact archive
5. Teaching roles + optional interactive studio disclosure
6. About / contact / CV / Scholar / GitHub
