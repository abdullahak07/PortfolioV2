01 · Research

Can an AI model truly forget?

My PhD treats machine unlearning as more than a single optimisation problem. The central question is whether information is actually removed—and whether that claim survives privacy attacks, representation analysis and deployment-time transformations.

01

Remove

Design selective forgetting methods that reduce the influence of targeted data without paying the full cost of retraining.

GradientHessianBoundary
02

Measure

Audit whether common forgetting, utility, privacy and representation metrics agree with an oracle retrained model.

UQSMIAAD / JS
03

Attack

Stress-test unlearned models after deployment transformations such as low-bit quantisation that may revive forgotten content.

INT4RecoveryRobustness
04

Generalise

Extend the problem into multimodal and vision-language systems, where information can persist across interacting modalities.

05 · About

A career across research, engineering and teaching.

I am a final-stage PhD researcher in Computer Science, university lecturer and Senior AI Engineer based in Perth. My research focuses on trustworthy and privacy-preserving AI, especially machine unlearning in neural, multimodal and vision-language systems.

Alongside research, I build practical AI and software systems—from reproducible experimentation pipelines and local-LLM products to interactive university teaching platforms and deployed web applications.

Outside the lab, I have led student communities as Founder & President of the Murdoch Cricket Club and President of the Murdoch University Muslim Students Association.
