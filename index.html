01 · Research focus

One question: what does it mean for a model to truly forget?

I study unlearning as a systems problem rather than a single metric: behaviour, privacy, internal representations and deployment robustness all matter.

01

Selective forgetting

Remove targeted information while protecting useful model capability and avoiding full retraining.

02

Representation audit

Test whether apparent output forgetting still leaves recoverable or identifiable internal structure.

03

Evaluation reliability

Study disagreement between forgetting, retention, privacy and representation metrics instead of trusting one score.

04

Deployment durability

Stress unlearned models after transformations such as low-bit quantisation to test whether forgotten information returns.
