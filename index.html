01 · Research thesis

Forgetting is not a single score.

My PhD asks whether information is actually removed from a model — and whether that claim survives internal representation analysis, privacy attacks and real deployment transformations.

01

Start with evidence

Measure the original model, forget set and retain set before making any claim about unlearning.

BaselineForget / retain
02

Remove selectively

Change the targeted behaviour while protecting useful capability — without paying the full cost of retraining.

GradientHessianBoundary
03

Audit beneath outputs

Output forgetting can coexist with retained internal structure. Evaluation has to inspect privacy, representation and oracle distance together.

MIAAD / JSOracle
04

Stress the deployment

A model that looks forgotten in the lab may change after low-bit quantisation or other transformations. Durability is part of the claim.
