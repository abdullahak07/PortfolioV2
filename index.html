01 · Research position

Forgetting is not a score. It is a claim that should survive behavioural, privacy, representation and deployment tests.

01

Forget selectively.

Remove targeted information while preserving useful model capability and avoiding full retraining.

02

Audit beneath outputs.

Test whether apparently forgotten information remains recoverable from internal representations.

03

Distrust single metrics.

Compare forgetting, retention, privacy and representation evidence rather than trusting one scalar.

04

Stress deployment.

Check whether transformations such as low-bit quantisation can make forgotten information reappear.

Alongside research, I build reproducible experimentation pipelines, applied AI systems and interactive teaching material.
