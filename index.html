Hi, I’m Abdullah

I am a final-stage PhD researcher in Computer Science at Murdoch University, working on machine unlearning, multimodal AI and trustworthy vision-language systems.

I’m particularly interested in selective forgetting, model auditing, recovery attacks and reliable evaluation. My work asks a simple question: when a model appears to forget, what evidence shows that the information is actually gone?

Alongside the PhD, I teach university courses across artificial intelligence, intelligent systems, cyber threat intelligence, network security and cryptography.

I also build reproducible research software, interactive teaching tools and applied AI systems that make technical ideas easier to inspect and use.
